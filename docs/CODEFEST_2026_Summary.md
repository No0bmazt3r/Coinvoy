## 2. Tracks

### Track 1: Healthtech
- **Problem:** Rising NCDs (about 1 in 5 adults has diabetes; hypertension growing) and fragmented health tools. Patients struggle to turn wellness data into habits.
- **Focus:** AI triage, digital wellness platforms, health literacy, biometric telemetry.
- **Tech:** AI/ML, Mobile App, NLP, IoT Software, Data Analytics.

### Track 2: Education
- **Problem:** One-size-fits-all digital materials lower retention; weak engagement in hybrid learning; poor school-to-career guidance.
- **Focus:** Adaptive learning engines, AI study companions, career navigators, gamified engagement.
- **Tech:** Web App, AI/ML, Gamification, NLP, PWA/Offline, Unity.

### Track 3: Civic Tech
- **Problem:** Fragmented rural public delivery, slow local authority response, no unified digital networks for public services.
- **Focus:** Live transit trackers, smart waste managers, flash flood alerts, agri-support, welfare wallets.
- **Tech:** GIS/Mapping, Mobile App, AI/ML, Open Data APIs.

### Track 4: Cybersecurity
- **Problem:** Cybercrime cases rose from 35,368 (2024) to 47,854 (Sep 2025). RM2.7 billion lost to scams in 2025. Older adults hit hardest.
- **Focus:** AI scam and phishing detection, digital literacy platforms, deepfake detection.
- **Tech:** Cybersecurity, AI/ML, Browser Extension, Python, Threat Intelligence.

### Track 5: Finance (Financial Inclusion)
- **Problem:** B40 families, gig workers and micro-SMEs struggle to access financing and manage digital finances, despite 63% digital finance penetration.
- **Focus:** Alternative financing, smart budgeting, AI financial literacy coaching, digital bookkeeping.
- **Tech:** Fintech, Web/Mobile App, AI/ML, Open Banking APIs, Blockchain (optional).
