# Code of Conduct

Coinvoy adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), the code of conduct used by most open-source projects. The full text there applies to everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## In short

**We pledge** to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Do:**
- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in their second language (BM or English).
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

**Don't:**
- Harass, insult or make personal or political attacks.
- Use sexualised language or imagery.
- Publish anyone's private information, including riders' data, without explicit permission.
- Do anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainers privately by email (see the maintainer's GitHub profile, [@No0bmazt3r](https://github.com/No0bmazt3r)). All reports are reviewed and handled in confidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful, following the enforcement guidelines in the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
